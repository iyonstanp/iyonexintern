Internship Policy

No internship fee. Ever.

Iyonex Automation provides internship opportunities to eligible students for practical industry exposure through engineering and research projects. The organization does not charge any internship fee from students. Selection is based purely on eligibility, project availability and mentor approval. Internship opportunities are announced based on company requirements.

Your details are sent securely to our team and are never shared. No internship fee is ever charged.
