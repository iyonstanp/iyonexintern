Move materials. Not people.

Iyonex Autonomous Material Movement Platform

We are developing an easy-to-use AMR platform for repetitive material transport in small and medium factories. The goal: straightforward mapping, destination selection and operator controls, without a specialist supervising every task.

DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

A practical roadmap for factory movement.

The features below describe our development direction. Availability and validation status must be confirmed for each pilot; they are not a claim of deployed functionality.

Autonomous Navigation

Robots plan and follow routes across the facility without fixed rails or wires.

Obstacle Detection

Sensors continuously scan the environment to identify people, equipment and obstructions.

Dynamic Path Planning

Routes are recalculated on the fly as the floor layout or traffic changes.

Point-to-Point Transportation

Materials move directly between defined pickup and drop-off locations.

Material Handling

Interfaces designed around how materials are actually loaded and unloaded on your floor.

Multi-Robot Coordination

Multiple units share the floor and coordinate to avoid conflicts and bottlenecks.

Real-Time Monitoring

Operators can see robot status, location and task progress as it happens.

Factory Workflow Integration

The platform is designed to connect with existing production and logistics workflows.

Scalable Deployment

Start with a single unit or route, and expand coverage as requirements grow.

Payload, dimensions, runtime, navigation accuracy and safety validation are not yet published. Discuss the current prototype and pilot fit with our team before making a procurement decision.

HOW IT OPERATES

Perception, planning and action, in a continuous loop.

An illustrative operating model for the platform under development.

01

Sense

Onboard sensors build a live picture of the surrounding floor, racks and moving obstacles.

02

Localize

The robot determines exactly where it is on the facility map in real time.

03

Plan

A path to the next pickup or drop-off point is calculated, accounting for traffic and obstacles.

04

Act

The robot moves, adjusting speed and route continuously as conditions change.

05

Report

Task status and location are reported back to the fleet monitoring layer.
