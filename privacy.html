PRIVACY

Your enquiry, handled clearly.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Enquiry information

Our enquiry forms prepare an email draft on your device. The website does not save form entries or resumes in a database or browser storage. Information reaches Iyonex only when you send the email yourself. You may contact us directly instead of using a form.

How we use information you send

We use your name, contact details, requirements and any resume you choose to send to respond to your enquiry, discuss a project or review your interest in a learning program. Please do not include passwords, identity documents or other information unrelated to your enquiry.

Third-party services

Your email provider handles the email draft and delivery under its own policies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and some illustrative images load from Unsplash; those requests disclose connection information to that provider. Hosting providers may keep operational access logs. External social links have their own privacy practices.

Questions or deletion requests

Email ceo@iyonex.com to ask about information you have sent, request a correction or request deletion. We retain correspondence as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting business relationship.
